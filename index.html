Start with a bank statement

Download your transactions as a CSV from your online banking, then import it here. Your data never leaves this browser — there's no server.

Where do I get a CSV?
  • Log in to your bank's internet banking on a computer (apps often only offer PDF).
  • Open the account's transaction history and pick the date range.
  • Look for Download or Export and choose CSV (sometimes labelled "Excel/CSV" or "comma separated").
  • Only see PDF? That's a statement, not a transaction export — check the transaction history page instead. PDF layouts vary too much to read reliably.
